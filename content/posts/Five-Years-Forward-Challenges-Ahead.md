Adeola, the Chief of the Public Defender's Office, discussed significant progress made over five years, including staff increases and the importance of in-person first appearances for clients. The group then focused on challenges within the criminal justice system, including funding issues, caseload management, and the need for better support and resources for public defenders in Travis County.
