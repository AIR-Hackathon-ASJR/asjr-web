Our own Bob Batlan was mentioned in a recent news story about Michael Center and his road back from prison. Center was caught up in the nationwide academic scandal and convicted of fraud for his part as a coach for the Texas Longhorns Tennis team.

“Bob Batlan befriended Center as both volunteered for Mobile Loaves, delivering food to the needy and cleaning the trucks. The 80-year-old former IBM worker also credited Center with helping him create a public defender’s office in Travis County to arrange for lawyers at a person’s arrest before they see a judge.

“Michael was really helpful,” Batlan said. “His case really helped us. Michael was coerced to plead guilty. He did some things wrong, but they bullied him, which is the nicest term for it. If people are worried about somebody working with kids, it shouldn’t be Michael you’re worried about. He’s one of the nicest, most caring persons I’ve ever met.”

Center earnestly wants readers to know that his isn’t a story of anger, regret and bitterness, but one of survival and redemption, hope and enlightenment. He’s adamant about that.

“I just want to help people,” he said.”
