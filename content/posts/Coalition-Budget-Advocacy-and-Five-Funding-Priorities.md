ASJR has been a member of the Coalition for Budget Accountability (CFBA) along with other advocacy groups , including Austin Area Urban League, Equity Action, Grassroots Leadership, Austin Civil Liberties and Change 1. Our goal has been to draft a budget proposal that shifts public dollars for the criminal /legal system, from punishment to prevention; to reduce incarceration; and to invest in holistic community-based care. We are working to strengthen and stabilize families impacted by the criminal/legal system by amplifying the voices of historically marginalized communities, educating them of their rights and advocating for fair and equitable legal representation.

We are prioritizing

competitive compensation to retain and expand legal representation for all individuals, especially those accused of felonies

the establishment of a funded oversight board

the expansion of mental/behavioral health diversion and deflection services 

prioritization of populations including residents with legal residency considerations, child/families impacted by the child welfare-juvenile legal systems, victims of crime, elder abuse, persons with disabilities

Information of rights and resources
